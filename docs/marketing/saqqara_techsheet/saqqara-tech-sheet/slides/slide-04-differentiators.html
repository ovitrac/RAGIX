What makes Saqqara unique

Five design choices that hold together

DifferentiatorWhat it meansValue
Provenance on every nodePage and position, cell, paragraph, slide or line, plus the reader and its versionEvery statement can be traced to its exact source
Rules that abstainOrdered rules over closed vocabularies; undecided cases are recorded, not filled inNo silent error presented as structure
No model insideReading and structure are computed; the only model is an optional embedderRuns anywhere, sovereign, nothing to host
One tree for every formatFive native formats, plus legacy Word and Excel through a recorded conversionOne citation scheme for a mixed corpus
Reproducible and accountedCanonical JSON, stable root hashes, every observation a node or a counted dropA reading can be replayed and audited
Four of the five are propositions of the specification, each carried by a test; the fifth, no model inside, is a design rule that can be read in the dependencies. The specification also publishes its two known gaps, H1 and H2.
