3. The capability catalogue

Saqqara's work divides into five capabilities, performed in order on every document. Each one marks what it read and what it inferred, and each one counts what it could not handle. An organisation can stop after reading, or go all the way to a searchable store and a structured reading report.

Five capabilities, from bytes to a citeable tree

ReadOpen PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Markdown files, and legacy Word and Excel through a recorded conversionRaw observations with their exact locators
BuildTurn those observations into one typed tree per documentEvery observation as a node, an attachment or a counted drop
RecogniseFind tables, header bands, label columns, value islands and sectionsInferred structure with its confidence, or an abstention

3.2 Recognising structure

After the tree is built, analyzers run in a fixed order. Each writes from a closed vocabulary, marks what it inferred, and abstains when its rules do not settle a case.

Analyzers, in the order a run applies them
AnalyzerWhat it recognises
tablesSegments spreadsheet blocks and types each one as a table, text or a list
grid tablesTypes Word and PowerPoint tables as a data form, a layout grid or uncertain
header bandsFinds a table's title, header band, label columns, section rows and core, or abstains; three header rows at most by default
islandsFlags disconnected islands of values, without re-cutting the table
sectionsCollects section names from eight reader channels, sets aside printed contents pages, orphan numbers and text repeated on three pages or more, and records each node's section ancestry
outline (opt-in)Promotes a numbered line to a heading only when it is a legal next step in the document's outline

Two points of scope. In a PDF, a standard run builds pages and paragraphs, not tables: PDF tables are read by the Explorer's table views, through the opt-in PyMuPDF reader (Section 3.5). In Markdown, a pipe table is kept as one paragraph with its raw lines. Three further analyzers are available as a library and are not applied by a standard run: headings shown only by type size or weight, the binding of captions to figures, and the grouping of drawn regions.

3.3 Figures, drawn regions and renderable regions

A document also shows things it does not say. With an asset store, the PDF reader records one figure per placement, keeps image bytes out of the tree in a store addressed by their hash, and counts what it skips. Drawn regions are identified by the hash of their drawing operators and extent, not by their pixels, and page furniture is excluded before grouping. Pages are rendered by pypdfium2 (Apache-2.0) at 150 dpi by default. Renderable regions give any line or cell its enclosing prose, table, list or figure as byte-identical JSON, with an optional PNG or JPEG image, within fixed size limits. A standard run does not yet produce figures, captions or drawn regions; this limit is stated in Saqqara's own documentation.

3.4 Indexing and search

The index kernel cuts each tree into chunks, with one roll-up per section, and writes them beside their documents into one SQLite file. A chunk's identity is a digest of its document, its level, its nodes and its text, so it does not change when nothing it depends on changes.

Lexical lane
SQLite FTS5 with BM25 ranking, accents folded. Every query term is quoted; a query with no searchable term is refused with a reason rather than answered with noise.
Dense lane, optional
Embeddings from the model the user configures (sentence-transformers, or Ollama on a host of their choice). With the default setting, no vector is written at all, not even a zero one, and the lane reports itself disabled.
Fusion
The two lanes are fused by reciprocal rank (k = 60). Each hit keeps its rank in both lanes and its final rank, so a reader can see why it came first.
Lifecycle
Documents can be moved to a trash, restored or purged. Texts the embedder rejects are isolated one by one and recorded in a table, never truncated silently.

3.5 The document Explorer

The Explorer reads a declared set of documents in four kernels (census, profile, reading, report) and adds field-level instruments: boundary-aware text views, bounded label-value windows, physical table views with column reconstruction, the header and row-label context of every cell, quantities read from that exact context, native-cell grid views with their holes and conflicts, and a ledger of which source characters each view covers. Personal stamps found on pages are masked by default. Its report replays the reading first and refuses to publish if anything differs. An optional model-based classification of profiles exists outside Saqqara and is disabled by default.

3.6 Everything that was not done is counted

A Saqqara run returns, beside the trees, a report of what it did not do: files refused, with the reason (unsupported format, unreadable file, converter unavailable); the abstention register; the drops of the builder and of each analyzer; the chunker's refusals; the embedder's refusals. The summary line of a run is computed from those registers, so it cannot claim more than was done.
